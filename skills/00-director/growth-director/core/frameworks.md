# Frameworks (how the Director thinks)

Use them silently. Name one to Gerrick only when it helps him decide.

## 1. Build, Distribute, Capture, Compound (the master lens)

Every opportunity is judged on all four. A move that only builds (content nobody sees) or only distributes (ads to nothing) is incomplete.

| Stage | Question | Atlas examples |
|---|---|---|
| Build | What valuable thing do we make? | ad families, carousels, UGC, Discovery Kit, a how-to guide |
| Distribute | How does it reach buyers? | TikTok organic, creators/affiliates, Meta and TikTok ads, Shopee ads, search |
| Capture | How does attention become a sale or an owned contact? | shop links, website checkout, WhatsApp, retention chat, email |
| Compound | How does this make the next cycle cheaper? | winning ad becomes a family, buyer videos become proof, buyers become affiliates, reviews lift conversion |

## 2. The constraint test (pick the one bottleneck)

Walk AARRR for each channel and stop at the first broken stage:
- **Acquisition:** are enough of the right people seeing us? (impressions, views, clicks)
- **Activation:** do they understand and click to the shop? (CTR, profile to shop taps)
- **Revenue:** do they buy? (conversion rate, AOV)
- **Retention:** do they come back? (repeat orders)
- **Referral:** do they bring others? (creator posts, shares, affiliates)

The earliest broken stage is the constraint. Fixing a later stage first is wasted effort.

## 3. ICE+ scoring (ranking opportunities)

Score 1 to 5 each: **Impact** on the KPI within 90 days, **Confidence** (evidence, not hope), **Ease** (cost plus Gerrick's minutes), **Compounding** (keeps producing after this week). Total out of 20. Recommend the top score unless an active initiative is mid-flight; finishing beats switching.

## 4. Leverage questions (ask before recommending)

- Is this the real problem, or a symptom?
- Is it growth or busywork?
- What is the cheapest way to test it this week?
- Can a skill in this repo do it without Gerrick?
- Does it leave an asset that keeps working?
- Is there a move that does the same thing with less money or time?

## 5. Content flywheel

One idea is never one post. A winning hook becomes: static ad variants (ad library), a carousel, a UGC clip, a TikTok slideshow, a Shopee listing image, a creator brief. Plan the whole set when the idea is approved; ship the cheapest first and promote whatever wins.

## 6. Experiment design

Every test has: hypothesis, single variable, KPI, budget cap, read date, and a scale or kill rule written before launch. Default rules for Atlas: read at day 3 for a red flag (spend with no clicks), decide at day 7; scale a winner in 20% steps; never judge on one day.

## 7. 80/20

Find the 20% of products, hooks, channels and creators making 80% of sales. Push those harder before inventing new ones (example: Amber, Tonka, Hedione, Iso E Super; Arabian-style notes).
