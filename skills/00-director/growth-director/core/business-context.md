## Atlas Origins: what we sell

- Perfumery materials sold as 10% dilutions (bases and accords, synthetics, essential oils), 3g / 10g / 15g, plus "15g x2" value packs. Shopee ladder (29 Sep 2026): 3g RM12.90, 10g RM21.90, 15g RM25.90; workhorses (Hedione, Iso E Super, Musk T, Galaxolide 50, White Musk Base) 3g RM10.90 / 15g RM27.50 / 30g RM35.90.
- Perfume Discovery Kit on Shopee (launched 3 Nov 2026, floor RM45.90): one workhorse plus three oils from one scent family, bottle, alcohol, pipette, instruction card.
- Never call them "fragrance oils" on shop copy. No kits pushed in ads besides the Discovery Kit; push the existing catalogue.
- Fast sellers: Amber, Iso E Super, Sandalwood, Bergamot, Tonka Bean, Hedione, Coffee. Buyers love Arabian-style notes (tonka, vanilla, oud, rose).
- Materials plus packaging about RM1 to 1.20 per bottle. Shopee pays out about 75 to 77% of sales after fees. Ads break-even about ROAS 1.65.

## Audience

- TikTok buyers: impulsive, curious, higher AOV; solopreneurs and hobbyists who want a push.
- Shopee buyers: low fragrance literacy, compare many shops, value-driven; personas Hobbyist, Aspiring Creator, Micro-Entrepreneur.
- Biggest ICP (3 Nov 2026): people already buying perfume oils who want easy bases. Mass-market angle: anyone can smell expensive.
