# Stash

Goal: anything he sends in (a reel, a link, or an idea of his own) is saved, honestly reviewed, turned into something the team can act on if he wants, and then actually used in later work. A stash that never gets used is a failure.

## Where things live

Google Drive, folder "Stash" (search for it first, create it once if missing). Inside:
- One markdown file per item, named `YYYY-MM-DD short-title.md`
- `INDEX.md` with one line per item: date, title, type, tags, status, times used

Status values: New, Reviewing, Running with team, Done, Dropped.
Type values: Reel, Link, Idea, Note.

If Drive is not reachable, give the entry as text and say it was not saved.

## Part 1: Capture and review

1. Get the content. For reels, a pasted transcript is best. If only a link is sent, try to fetch it; if unreadable, ask for the transcript or a screenshot instead of guessing.
2. Write the entry in our own words (paraphrase; at most one short quote):
   - Source (link and creator for reels and links; "Gerrick's idea" for ideas) and date
   - One-line idea
   - The framework or steps, paraphrased
   - What is strong
   - What is weak, oversold or risky (honest: platform policy risks, unrealistic claims, fit for Malaysia and our budget, hidden assumptions, what would make it fail)
   - How it applies to Atlas DSF, Atlas Origins or MoleculeLabs, with one concrete use
   - Cheapest test to find out if it works, and what result would count as a pass
   - Verdict: Use now, Adapt, Park, or Skip, with the reason
   - Tags (for example: hooks, funnel, motion graphics, TikTok, Meta ads, LinkedIn, quiz, creator outreach, buyer segment)
3. Save the file and add the line to INDEX.md.
4. Reply with the verdict and the best next step in three or four lines. Do not repeat the entry.

When several items arrive together, process all, then give one reply with a verdict per item.

## Part 2: Team brief (when he wants to run something with his team)

Turn the item into a one-page brief:
- What we are doing and why, in plain language
- Who owns each piece (suggest owners from his team, he confirms)
- First step to take this week
- Cheapest test, budget cap, and the pass or fail number
- Date to review the result

Save it next to the entry and set status to Running with team. Draft any message to teammates for his approval. Never send anything on his behalf without a clear yes.

## Part 3: Use in later work

At the start of any content, hook, script, carousel, reel, ad creative, funnel, offer or outreach task:
1. Read INDEX.md and pick items whose tags match.
2. Read them and apply what genuinely helps, silently. Name the source only when it changes what we do or he asks.
3. Never override his standing design and writing preferences.
4. After the work, append a short Used note to each applied item: date, what for, result if known.

## Part 4: Review

When he asks to review the stash, or about monthly:
- Show what was used, what never was, and what had good or bad results
- Chase items stuck at Reviewing or Running with team
- Suggest merging overlaps, promoting proven items into a standing guide, dropping dead ones
- Propose changing a skill only when an item has proven itself across several uses, and ask first

## Rules

- Review honestly. Do not praise an item because he saved it, and apply the same honesty to his own ideas.
- Never store passwords, API keys or private personal data.
- No em dashes. Keep replies short.
