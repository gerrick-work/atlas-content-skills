# TikTok caption and sound picker

Use when Gerrick asks for a caption, hook, hashtags or music for a TikTok photo carousel or reel.

## Step 1: Know the post
Ask or infer: brand (Atlas Origins B2C or Atlas DSF B2B), format (carousel or reel), the one point of the post, and whether it is organic or going to be boosted as an ad.

## Step 2: Write 3 caption options
Rules drawn from the TikTok Ads Library analysis (3,326 product ads, winners = top 20% within category):
- Lead with a real offer or the strongest result, not a tutorial intro. Only state offers that are real.
- Ads: call to action is a click (WhatsApp or shop link), not 'comment a word'. Organic: comments are fine.
- 2 hashtags or fewer.
- Never compare our scent to a designer, luxury or niche perfume.
- No empty claims like 'long-lasting' or 'smells amazing' with nothing specific behind them.
- No fake scarcity ('limited stock', fake deadlines).
- Gift and occasion hooks (Raya, National Day, birthday) and genuine questions are worth testing for fragrance.
- Caption length, emojis and question marks showed no effect, so keep it short and natural.
- Voice: unhinged but factually accurate, faceless, never name other brands.
- Gerrick's style: no em dashes, warm and plain wording.

Caveat: these findings come from paid ads, so they inform hooks and offers more than organic caption mechanics. The CTR-based ranking was never re-validated.

## Step 3: Pick sounds
1. Search the web for the current weekly TikTok trending sounds (New Engen publishes a weekly list on Mondays) and check TikTok Creative Center for Malaysia.
2. Match mood to the post: soft, dreamy, reveal-style posts suit slower tracks; fast-cut edits suit high-energy tracks.
3. Shortlist 3 sounds with the reason each fits.
4. Remind him that business accounts can only use tracks in the Commercial Music Library, so confirm each track is available in the in-app 'Add sound' search before promising it. If not, use an original sound or a library track with a similar mood.

## Step 4: Output
Give 3 captions, 2 hashtag options, and 3 sound options in plain chat text (no code block, so it pastes cleanly into WhatsApp or TikTok).
