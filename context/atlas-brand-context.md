# Atlas brand context (read first, every skill)

Every skill in this repo assumes these rules. When a skill and this file disagree, the newer dated rule wins; flag the clash to Gerrick so the repo gets fixed.

## Writing rules (both brands)

- No em dashes, ever. Rewrite the sentence instead of swapping in another dash.
- Plain active sentences. No self-elevating or passive phrasing.
- Only claims Atlas can defend today. No invented figures, reviews, client names, sales counts or fake deadlines. Prices come from the live shop.
- Never reveal stock, suppliers or tools. Atlas owns its formulas; clients get exclusive use.
- Repurpose structure, never wording. Go cross-industry for ideas; never copy another creator's lines.
- Angles should be strange-but-relevant, not generic ("why your perfume smells bland" is the kind of thing to avoid).

## Visual rules

- Never grey backgrounds; pure white instead. Avoid big empty white space (reads as AI).
- Origins: corporate "A" mark + ATLAS ORIGINS lockup, large, centred at the top of every ad. Ad colours white, saffron `#D8A23F`, rose `#E2A091`, green `#22473A` (meme template only). Origins intro carousels use the flat green `#2D5C43` and lemon `#F6CE45` system, rectangles not pills, yellow series tag top-right.
- DSF: white-first, Atlas green `#2C463A` always as a gradient, text only black, white or green, one accent (saffron or rose clay) on one page per post.
- Social text at least 60 to 100px from every edge (70px on ads). Only the headline bold.
- Headlines centred on every slide (label above and sub-line under centred too). CTA buttons have fully rounded pill ends; tags and boxes stay rectangles (11 Nov 2026).
- Imagery as transparent cut-outs unless the photo is the background. Rich context (setting, people, telling objects), never a lone symbol.
- 1 to 2 simple diagrams in every carousel; never AI-generated diagrams. Icons and small objects come from the 3D icon library (skills/03-static/icon-library), never drawn by Claude (11 Nov 2026). Logos and illustrations go to an image model via OpenRouter, not hand-drawn by Claude.
- Decks and proposal PDFs: green only for a real call to action, few words per slide.

## Creative standard

- Before making any post, carousel, reel, ad or LinkedIn image, read context/creative-tastebook.md and start from one of its patterns. Reference slides: context/tastebook/img/ and https://claude.ai/artifact/HGt2sno2xtueia8cH5JkiH (11 Nov 2026).

## People and AI

- Never real people, celebrities, creators' faces or other brands' characters. Gerrick's face and voice never appear.
- AI personas allowed for skits only, with the platform's AI label; never a fake customer testimonial.
